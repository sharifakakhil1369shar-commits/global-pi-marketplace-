# Global Pi Marketplace
## PROJECT MASTER PLAN

### 1. Project Vision
Build a real, secure, scalable and globally accessible marketplace within the Pi Network ecosystem.

The platform will connect customers, businesses, hotels, restaurants, shops and service providers worldwide.

The long-term goal is a fully operational international website accessible from mobile phones and computers.

### 2. Core Features — MVP
- Customer registration and login
- Country and city selection
- Business registration and listings
- Business profiles
- Search and categories
- Reservation and order requests
- Business owner dashboard
- Central Super Admin dashboard
- Customer support and complaints
- English language initially

### 3. User Roles
- Super Admin
- Business Owner
- Customer
- Support Staff

Each business will manage its own orders and reservations. The Super Admin will supervise the entire platform.

### 4. Orders and Reservations
- Customers submit requests.
- Requests go directly to the relevant business.
- Business owners confirm availability and prices.
- Initial payments go directly to businesses.
- Platform commissions are recorded separately.
- Central payments and automatic commissions are future features.

### 5. Revenue Model
Potential revenue sources:
- Business subscriptions
- Advertising
- Premium business services
- Order and reservation commissions

Revenue is not guaranteed and depends on actual platform activity.

### 6. Pi Network Integration
- Review official Pi Developer documentation.
- Integrate Pi SDK when technically and officially supported.
- Test Pi authentication.
- Enable Pi payments only after proper testing and compliance.
- Never store wallet passphrases or private keys on the platform.

### 7. Financial Structure
Plan for:
- Multi-currency accounting
- Business payment records
- Platform revenue and commissions
- Pi wallet integration when permitted
- Financial reporting dashboard

### 8. International Expansion
Initial project management will operate from Afghanistan.

Future possibility:
Establishing a legal company or financial center in a suitable country such as UAE, Türkiye or a European country.

This is a future possibility, not a current commitment. No foreign company registration expenses should be made at this stage.

### 9. Security and Privacy
- Protect customer and business information.
- Use secure authentication.
- Never publish passwords, API secrets or private keys.
- Add appropriate privacy policies and terms of service.
- Keep the public GitHub repository free of confidential information.

### 10. Development Priorities

#### Essential — MVP
Build and test the basic marketplace, registration, business profiles, search, orders, reservations and administration.

#### Next Stage
Add advanced dashboards, multilingual support, verified businesses, improved financial reporting and additional payment options.

#### Future Planning
International company registration, travel ticket services, automated commissions, advanced analytics and global expansion.

## 15. Business Management and Platform Structure

### Super Admin
The central administrator manages and supervises the entire platform.

### Business Owners
Each business has its own independent account and dashboard.

### Customers
Customers can search, submit orders and request reservations.

### Support Staff
Support staff help customers and review complaints with limited permissions.

### Business Operations
- Each business receives its own orders and reservation requests.
- Business owners manage prices, availability and confirmations.
- The central administrator supervises platform activity.
- Businesses are responsible for responding to their own customers.
- The platform manages complaints and monitors business conduct.

### Initial Payment Model
Customers pay businesses directly.
Platform commissions are recorded separately.
Centralized payments and automatic commission collection are future developments.
